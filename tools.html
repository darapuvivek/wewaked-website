These are deep links, not account connections. This site is a set of static files with no server behind it, so there is nowhere to safely hold an OAuth secret or your tokens — which means it cannot genuinely sign in to Gmail, Slack or Teams and read or post on your behalf. What the buttons above do is real and immediate: they hand the selected note to the app you already have open. Proper two-way sync would need a small backend, which I have not built.
